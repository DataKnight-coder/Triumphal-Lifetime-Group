# Brand Tokens

## Master Colours
- **Midnight:** `#030713` (Dominant dark)
- **Emerald:** `#092A19` (Secondary strong)
- **Signature Gold:** `#CE9129` (Primary accent)
- **Heritage Gold:** `#DBAE57` (Secondary accent)
- **Champagne:** `#F7CB64` (Highlight)
- **Ivory:** `#F7F5EF` (Dominant light background)
- **Stone:** `#EEEEEE` (Secondary light background)
- **Charcoal:** `#181A1C` (Text/UI dark)
- **White:** `#FFFFFF` (Clean background)

*Master Visual Hierarchy: Midnight + Gold + Ivory*

## Typography
- **Display / Editorial:** `Cormorant Garamond` (Used for H1, H2, pull quotes, major campaign statements)
- **Interface / Body:** `Manrope` (Used for navigation, buttons, forms, cards, body content, labels, UI)

## Visual Direction Elements
- Large editorial typography and strong photography
- Generous whitespace and architectural layouts
- Dark cinematic sections with thin gold detailing
- Subtle, refined motion (e.g., hover scale, masked reveals)
- Asymmetric compositions for storytelling
- Full-width image storytelling and large service cards
- Premium hover transitions
