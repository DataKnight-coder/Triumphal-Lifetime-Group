Rofiat Olabisi Akilapa leads the Group’s Global Education Advisory function, supporting prospective students as they explore international education opportunities. Her role focuses on student profile assessment, institution and programme selection, application coordination, documentation guidance and admission follow-up.

She is committed to helping students make informed decisions about their education journey while providing professional and personalised support throughout the application process. Admission, scholarship and visa decisions remain solely with the relevant educational institutions and government authorities.
