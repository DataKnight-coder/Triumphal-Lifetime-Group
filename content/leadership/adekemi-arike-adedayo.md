Adekemi Arike Adedayo is the Founder and Group Chief Executive Officer of Triumphal Lifetime Group, providing strategic leadership and direction across the Group’s business and social-impact activities. She leads the Group’s vision for sustainable growth, innovation, and responsible business development.

With extensive experience in business leadership and human resources consulting, she brings expertise in people management, organisational development, business strategy, and entrepreneurship. Her leadership approach focuses on building effective systems, developing people, strengthening organisations, and creating practical solutions that support businesses and communities.

She is also the Founder of Triumphal Lifetime Charity Foundation, reflecting her commitment to community development and humanitarian initiatives.

### Education & Professional Qualifications

* BA (Hons) International Business Management — First Class Honours, University of Hertfordshire, United Kingdom
* BSc Economics, Imo State University, Nigeria
* CHRP, CHRM & CHRC, American Certification Institute
