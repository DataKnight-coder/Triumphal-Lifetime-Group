<p>As Group CEO, she provides strategic leadership across the Group, shaping its vision, corporate strategy, governance, partnerships, and long-term growth. <p>Her leadership is grounded in integrity, innovation, professionalism, and service, with a commitment to ensuring that each company maintains its distinct purpose while contributing to the Group's shared vision and long-term value creation.</p>
