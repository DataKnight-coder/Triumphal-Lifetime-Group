# TLG client confirmations

Do not publish or infer the following until the client supplies evidence and approval.

- Exact legal and registration structure, contracting entity, data controller, governing law and jurisdiction.
- Founding date/history and any expansion chronology.
- Nigeria, UAE and Canada location status, exact address, approved public label, services offered and whether clients may attend.
- Final Real Estate director and final titles/biographies for every leader; documentary evidence for qualifications.
- Permission to publish LinkedIn URLs, portraits and professional credentials.
- Formal university, regulator, government, developer, financial-institution, partner or affiliate relationships and logo-use permission.
- Recruitment authority by jurisdiction, genuine vacancies, CV destination, retention period and applicant privacy wording.
- Foundation programme and completed-impact evidence: activity, place, year, description, consented genuine photographs, and fundraising authority.
- Digital-product readiness, files, names, prices, access/delivery, payments, cancellations, refunds and legal terms.
- Advanced IT capabilities, certifications, security/cloud scope and vendor partnerships.
- Enquiry destination mailbox, Cloudflare Turnstile site/secret keys, permitted production origin and retention period.
- Business-owned analytics platform, property/consent configuration and measurement ID.
- Final legal review of Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer, mobility, property, recruitment and fundraising language.

The implementation leaves unverified locations and Foundation impact empty, keeps disputed leadership inactive, and keeps high-trust Insights in draft.
